# Philippines boundary

Source: geoBoundaries gbOpen, PHL-ADM0-24100683, simplified ADM0, boundary year 2020.
Original data: OCHA Philippines and National Mapping and Resource Information Authority.

Source: https://data.humdata.org/dataset/philippines-administrative-levels-0-to-3

Bundled for offline point-in-polygon country validation, not navigation or maritime/legal boundaries.
The downloaded simplified geometry is minified, with coordinates rounded to five decimal places (roughly one metre); vertices are retained.
Coastal and small-island points not covered by this land geometry may instead be confirmed by provider country information. A provider failure preserves the pin draft; manual text-only planning remains available.
